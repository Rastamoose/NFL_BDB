We quantify **hidden off-ball receiver impact** with **Traffic Separation Gain**: the yards of separation a target gains in the 0.5 seconds after a teammate creates nearby defensive traffic.
Using 10 Hz player tracking, we identify close supporter–defender encounters and measure the target’s observed separation before and after.
A lightweight model trained on no-traffic moments estimates expected movement, highlighting unusually effective traffic without claiming a designed rub or causal effect.
This creates a simple, broadcast-ready stat that credits the unsung receiver who helped open the play without ever touching the ball.
